## What I'm building

### [Arroway](https://arroway.app)

Keeps people and their AIs on the same page. When a team works with Claude, ChatGPT, Codex and Cursor at the same time, the work gets lost between them: each new session starts from zero and someone has to explain everything again. Arroway records what each AI did and what was handed over, and its manager agent knows the goal, decides who picks up the next piece of work, follows up on what stopped halfway and brings the real decisions back to a person. It plugs into Claude Code, Cowork and Cursor through the [Arroway plugin](https://github.com/oaleviola/arroway-plugin).

### [MemberVIP](https://membervip.com.br)

Infrastructure for professional content creators in Brazil: management, sales and content delivery on autopilot. Each creator gets an AI that sells and chats with fans in her own voice, while payments, access and delivery run on their own. More than 5,000 memberships sold and 170,000 messages exchanged so far.

### [sayurname](https://sayurname.com)

A GEO agency: it gets companies recommended when their customers ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity or Claude. We measure the four AIs every week, publish content every month and make the site easy for agents to find and use. It started as the work I did for MemberVIP, where 44% of sign-ups over 90 days came from ChatGPT.

### [Arxi Global](https://arxiglobal.com) · cofounder

International business, financial and wealth planning for companies and partners who want to grow beyond Brazil and protect what they have built. The ARXI agent runs the diagnosis, compares structures and scenarios, and connects the client to the specialists who execute.
